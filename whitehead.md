# Whitehead foundations

Whitehead's later turn to experience and perception was not a break from his algebra and logic but its widening: one method, applied to more and more domains. That widening is the philosophical model for this series.

| Period | Main works | Domain | What carries over |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Algebra and logic | *A Treatise on Universal Algebra* (1898); *Principia Mathematica*, with Russell (1910–13) | Formal systems | Structure generalized beyond quantity: a calculus can govern any domain that satisfies its rules |
| Philosophy of nature | *Principles of Natural Knowledge* (1919); *The Concept of Nature* (1920); *The Principle of Relativity* (1922) | Physics and perception | Extensive abstraction: points and instants built logically out of experienced events |
| Metaphysics | *Science and the Modern World* (1925); *Symbolism* (1927); *Process and Reality* (1929) | Experience, perception, value | One process scheme for physical events and experience alike |
| Late work | *Adventures of Ideas* (1933); *Modes of Thought* (1938) | Civilization, beauty, understanding | Aesthetics and value as features of process |

## 1. Against the bifurcation of nature

In *The Concept of Nature* Whitehead rejected splitting nature into a causal world of physics and a perceived world of color and sound. Physics and cognition are one process seen from two sides. This is the foundation of the [thesis](thesis.md).

## 2. Two modes of perception (*Symbolism*, 1927)

- **Causal efficacy:** the vague, heavy, bodily sense of being affected by the world.
- **Presentational immediacy:** the clear, distinct display of colors and shapes in space.

Ordinary perception joins them through symbolic reference. This is the explanatory layer under the meta-model:

| Mechanism | In Whitehead's terms |
| --- | --- |
| Perceptual lag (Beethoven, Van Gogh) | Energy is first felt through causal efficacy (bass pressure, compressed yellow), then reaches presentational immediacy as a clear event |
| Bypass (Scriabin, Van Gogh) | The work aims directly at causal efficacy, delivering felt force before clear representation |
| Concealed control (Furtwängler) | Announcing the next move hands experience to presentational immediacy, and the felt force is lost |

## 3. Intensity through contrast (*Process and Reality*)

Depth of experience comes from contrasts held together without eliminating either side: the metaphysical form of the [Shakespeare operator](figures/shakespeare.md). Scriabin's fusion is the limiting case, where contrast resolves into unity.

## 4. Criteria for the series

*Process and Reality* asks that a scheme of ideas be **coherent and logical** (rational side) and **applicable and adequate** (empirical side): able, in the end, to interpret every element of experience. Each new figure added here should be tested against these four criteria.

## Comparators

Russell moved toward analytic narrowing. Bergson shared process and lived time but distrusted mathematical form. Peirce is the true peer, joining logic, semiotics, science and cosmology; Peirce's semiotics and Whitehead's symbolic reference are close relatives. Whitehead went further in building physics and the theory of perception into one formal scheme.
