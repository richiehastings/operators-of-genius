# From logic to neurons: Peirce, Carnap and brain–computer interfaces

Logic became the circuitry of machines, then the first model of the brain, and was then outgrown. Today's brain–computer interfaces (BCIs) read dynamic patterns of neural activity, not logical propositions, a result that favors Whitehead's process view over Carnap's logical construction.

## 1. Logic → circuits → neurons (1886–1945)

| Year | Figure | Step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1886 | Peirce, letter to Allan Marquand | Proposes building logic machines with electrical circuits |
| 1928 | Carnap, *Der logische Aufbau der Welt* | Constructs the known world logically from "elementary experiences" |
| 1937 | Shannon, master's thesis | Boolean algebra describes switching circuits exactly |
| 1943 | McCulloch & Pitts, "A Logical Calculus of the Ideas Immanent in Nervous Activity" | Neurons as logic gates, built on Carnap's *Logical Syntax* and *Principia* notation (Pitts studied with Carnap) |
| 1945 | Von Neumann, EDVAC report | Uses McCulloch–Pitts neurons to describe computer design |

For a moment, *Principia*'s logic, Carnap's syntax, the electrical circuit, the neuron and the computer were one formalism.

## 2. The turn: the brain is not a logic machine

Real neurons fire in noisy, analog, time-dependent patterns; meaning lives in the coordinated activity of populations over time. Movement planning is described as a trajectory through neural state space: a dynamic form unfolding, not a proposition evaluated. Logic is a coarse projection of something more basic, process and dynamic form: Whitehead's view, with Clynes's essentic forms in the same family. The brain computes in energy contours over time, the invariant the operators track in Beethoven and Scriabin.

## 3. Now (as of November 2026)

- **Movement.** Neuralink's Telepathy reads hand-movement areas of motor cortex and converts the signals into digital commands; 21 participants in early 2026, at least 26 by mid-2026. One participant controls a robotic arm to eat independently.
- **Art.** A participant creates digital art by thought and plans a physical gallery.
- **Speech.** Neuralink's VOICE trial targets real-time speech restoration. Paradromics reported (September 2026) zero-error decoding of a patient's spontaneous speech, including a live phone call. UC Davis researchers report thought-to-speech synthesis in about 10 milliseconds.
- **Writing into the brain.** The University of Pittsburgh provides sensory feedback through a BCI. Neuralink's Blindsight vision implant had no registered study on ClinicalTrials.gov as of November 2, 2026.

Early results, small numbers of participants, much of the reporting from the companies themselves.

## 4. Synthesis with the operators

| Operator / concept | BCI correspondence |
| --- | --- |
| Thesis (translators) | The BCI is a new translator: neural dynamic form → cursor, grasp, speech, art. The first medium fed directly by the nervous system |
| Bypass | Made literal: intention → decoder → action, skipping spinal cord and muscles. Art produced by thought is bypass in its purest form |
| Perceptual lag | At ~10 ms, synthesized speech feels like one's own; agency research suggests longer latency makes output feel done *to* the user. Timing decides ownership |
| Mozart's conversation | Brain and decoder co-adapt: two participants in a bounded exchange converging on balance |
| Peirce's sign | Spike pattern = sign; intended movement = object; decoder output = interpretant. Meaning arises in interpretation |
| Whitehead's two modes | *Speculative, testable:* cortical stimulation delivers presentational immediacy (phosphene, localized touch) without bodily causal efficacy; Whitehead predicts it will feel thin or unreal until the bodily layer is restored |

## 5. The larger arc

1. **1890s–1940s:** thought modeled as logic; logic built as electricity (Peirce, Carnap, Shannon, McCulloch–Pitts).
2. **1950s–2010s:** the brain computes in dynamic form, not propositions (Whitehead and Clynes vindicated in principle).
3. **Now:** machines read and write those dynamic forms directly.

The great artists were the first engineers of this problem: how to transmit a dynamic form from one nervous system to another, through a medium, with minimal loss. The artist encoded it in sound or paint; the BCI reads it from neurons. The universal language of the [thesis](thesis.md) may be the language of neural dynamics itself.

**Cautions.** Neural data raises serious privacy and consent questions as devices scale. "Thought equals circuit" claims in the McCulloch–Pitts style should remain historical, not current.
