# Thesis: one physics, many translators

Physics runs neutrally through every medium. Each art form is a translator that converts a common underlying dynamic (energy, tension, motion, release) into a particular material: sound, color, language, gesture. Cognition is not a passive receiver but the place of synthesis, where physics becomes experience. The underlying "something" being translated is not yet clearly understood.

The operators in this repository are case studies of this thesis: each describes how one translator handles the same dynamic. Mozart balances it, Beethoven compresses it, Shakespeare splits it into opposites, Scriabin and Van Gogh shorten its path, and Furtwängler re-creates it in real time.

## Grounding

- **Whitehead.** Process philosophy treats experience as a mode of physical process ("prehension"), not something apart from it, and rejects the "bifurcation of nature" into physical cause and perceived quality. The art work is a structured event in which physical process becomes felt experience. See [whitehead.md](whitehead.md).
- **Clynes.** Essentic forms: one emotional dynamic shape, expressible through touch, voice or music and recognizable across them. A candidate for the universal language itself.
- **Langer.** Music shares the morphology of feeling. Form, not content, is what carries across media.
- **Scriabin's *Prometheus* (1910)** scored a light keyboard (clavier à lumières) to translate one dynamic into sound and color at once.
- **Cross-modal research.** Music–color associations are consistent and mediated by emotion (Palmer, Schloss et al., 2013): translation between media passes through an emotional-dynamic middle layer.

## Two qualifications

- **Cognition is not linear.** Hearing does not register energy proportionally (Fletcher–Munson), and perception follows logarithmic laws (Weber–Fechner). Every translator must account for cognition's transfer function, so the universal language is a synthesis of physics and cognition, not pure physics. Genius includes intuitive knowledge of that transfer function.
- **Universal substrate, learned codes.** The universal layer is the dynamic contour shared through physics and the nervous system. Cultural vocabularies built on it are dialects.

## The open question: what is invariant under translation?

| Candidate invariant | Operator that governs it |
| --- | --- |
| Energy contour: build-up, peak, release over time or space | Beethoven |
| Ratio relations: proportion, harmonic-series intervals, balance | Mozart |
| Number of opposed forces: one kernel, a pair, many participants | Shakespeare, Mozart |
| Path length between source and perceiver | Scriabin, Van Gogh |
