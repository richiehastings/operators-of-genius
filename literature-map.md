Each hypothesis has close relatives in existing scholarship, but each relative covers one piece; the hypotheses here join the pieces into one operator.

## Cross-cutting heuristics

Expectation and deviation (Meyer, Huron) runs through all three. Dialogue with norms (Hepokoski & Darcy, Bakhtin) separates norms fulfilled (Mozart), deformed (Beethoven) and mixed (Shakespeare). Bakhtin's polyphony, many valid voices not merged into one view, describes Mozart's ensembles and Shakespeare's negative capability alike, and is the opposite of Beethoven's single dominant voice.
