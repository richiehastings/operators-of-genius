# Mozart: conversation within a bounded domain

**Hypothesis.** Mozart's foundational operator is conversation among defined participants, inside a formal boundary strict enough to contain the conversation and force a balanced conclusion. The mechanism is obvious in the operas and persists in the concertos and instrumental works.

## The operator

- **Domain:** sonata form as a tonal space with a fixed tonic and a required return. Rosen's "sonata principle" is the boundary rule: material stated outside the tonic must be resolved into it.
- **Participants:** a theme paired with an instrument. Themes carry social identities (march, aria, minuet, hunt, learned style), as topic theory shows.
- **Operator:** imitation, answer, interruption, completing another's phrase, contradiction by mode or key.
- **Closure:** every participant brought within the tonic.
- **Self-similarity:** the antecedent–consequent phrase (question, answer) is the atom, repeated at every scale.

## Evidence

- **Concertos.** In K. 271 (1777) the piano answers the orchestra in bar 2. In K. 466 the soloist enters with a theme the orchestra never plays. From K. 450 on, winds are a third party, not color. K. 488 swaps oboes for clarinets to suit its character.
- **The cadenza** is free speech inside one suspended cadence: the soliloquy happens within a boundary whose end is already fixed.
- **Sinfonia Concertante, K. 364.** The viola is tuned up a semitone so it can converse as the violin's equal.
- **Chamber music.** The "Haydn" Quartets give every voice real lines; K. 452 treats five unlike instruments as equals.
- **"Jupiter" finale, K. 551.** Five themes combine in invertible counterpoint: the operatic ensemble without words.

## Two kinds of closure

- *Reconciliation:* major-key works; Figaro's "Contessa, perdono."
- *Absorption:* minor-key works such as K. 550 and K. 491, where the lyrical theme is forced into the minor.

Don Giovanni is the limit case: a participant who refuses the conversation is expelled.

## Process

Mozart's model was generative. He wrote a short-score skeleton (melody, bass, cues) and filled it in later. His roughly 150 fragments suggest the model acted as a gate at the point of entry: openings that didn't fit were abandoned, not repaired. The heavily corrected "Haydn" Quartets, which he called the fruit of long and laborious effort, are the exception that fits the rule: the more equal the participants, the more computation the conversation needed.

## Contrast

Haydn tends to develop one idea with wit. Beethoven strains the boundary. Mozart keeps many distinct characters inside a boundary he never breaks.
