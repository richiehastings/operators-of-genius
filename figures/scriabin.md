# The bypass operator: Scriabin (with Van Gogh)

**Hypothesis (Richard).** The artist sometimes tries to bypass human perceptual bias by shortening the path from upstream sources of inspiration and energy to the downstream artistic output. Van Gogh paints sunflowers and stars as if directly from the energetic source. Scriabin's *The Poem of Ecstasy* does not evoke sexuality through a mediated model; it enacts it, as if sexual interaction could occur through the physics of auditory experience.

## Two chains

- *Representation:* source → appearance → depiction → recognition → inferred energy. Energy arrives last, by inference.
- *Enactment:* the source's dynamic form → the work's dynamic form → the perceiver's nervous system. Energy arrives first, before the subject is named.

## Scriabin, *Le Poème de l'extase*, Op. 54 (1905–08)

- First to be titled *Poème orgiaque*; its roughly 300-line companion poem is overtly sexual.
- The music follows the contour of arousal: waves of rising tension that fall back before their peak, each higher, ending in a long C major climax with full orchestra and organ.
- **Mechanism.** Langer: music shares the morphology of feeling. Clynes: essentic forms produce the emotion by sharing its shape.
- **Neural evidence.** Studies of musical chills (Blood & Zatorre, 2001) and dopamine release at musical peaks (Salimpoor et al., 2011) show music engages the brain's reward circuitry directly.

**Limit of the identity claim.** Scriabin fused the erotic, the creative and the mystical; his poem originally ended "I am God," later softened to "I am." The partner is ultimately the Self or cosmos. Scriabin seeks fusion where Shakespeare holds duality: a possible counter-type.

## Van Gogh

In his 1888 letters he says he used color arbitrarily to express himself forcefully rather than reproduce appearance. What he bypasses is appearance, not the human hand: the impasto stroke makes the hand more visible, and *The Starry Night* was painted largely from memory. The path shortens between energy and viewer, not between object and canvas. See [van-gogh.md](van-gogh.md).

## The paradox of the shortened path

A shorter perceived path requires more artistic mediation, not less: Scriabin's orchestra and post-Wagnerian harmony, Van Gogh's systematic complementary color. *Ars est celare artem.*

> The artist minimizes the perceived transformations between source energy and audience by maximizing the precision of the actual transformations.

## Compression and bypass coexist

Scriabin's waves are Beethoven-style compressions. Compression controls *when* energy arrives; bypass controls *how many steps* it passes through.
