# Shakespeare: paired opposition across scale

**Hypothesis.** An emotion, perception or experience is always composed as a duality, and the duality decays into further pairs. Each experience can be singular or collective, interchangeably. Something growing and enjoyable to a collective can at the same moment be ephemeral, disappointing and sad within a single condition.

## The operator

1. **Duality:** E = (a, ā), two opposed affects held together.
2. **Decay into pairs:** each pole splits again, a → (a₁, ā₁): a recursive binary tree.
3. **Scale interchange:** one voice can stand for a group; a group's experience can narrow to one person.
4. **Concurrent opposition across scale:** E(collective) = + while E(singular) = − at the same moment.

## Evidence

- **Oxymoron as the atom.** Romeo: "O brawling love! O loving hate! … Feather of lead, bright smoke, cold fire, sick health!" Juliet: "Parting is such sweet sorrow."
- **Stated outright, *Hamlet* 1.2.** Claudius: "With mirth in funeral and with dirge in marriage, / In equal scale weighing delight and dole." The court celebrates while Hamlet alone mourns.
- **Stated outright, *King Lear* 5.3.** Gloucester's heart, "'Twixt two extremes of passion, joy and grief, / Burst smilingly."
- **Temporal decay, Sonnet 129.** "A bliss in proof, and proved, a very woe; / Before, a joy proposed; behind, a dream."
- **Collective joy declared ephemeral, *The Tempest* 4.1.** Prospero halts a wedding masque: "Our revels now are ended … We are such stuff / As dreams are made on."
- **Comic endings.** Group joy with one figure outside it: Malvolio (*Twelfth Night*), Shylock and Antonio (*Merchant*), Jaques (*As You Like It*), the postponed marriages of *Love's Labour's Lost*, Falstaff rejected at Hal's coronation (*Henry IV, Part 2*).
- **Joy becoming loss, *The Winter's Tale* 5.2.** Leontes finds his daughter, "as if that joy were now become a loss."

## Simultaneity

Shakespeare achieves simultaneity through dramatic irony: the singular character feels one affect while the collective audience feels its opposite. Mozart does it within one sound; Shakespeare across the edge of the stage.

## Qualifications

- "At all times" is unfalsifiable as written. Testable form: peak moments almost never present a pure affect, and pure affect is usually marked as a limitation (Malvolio's self-love, Angelo's rigid virtue).
- Decay is sometimes triadic (Lear's daughters, the three caskets, the witches), but these usually reduce to a pair.

## Refinement: a conservation principle

When a collective reaches joy, the displaced sorrow is not destroyed; it concentrates in a remainder, usually one singular figure who carries it out of the frame. In the tragedies the inverse holds: singular catastrophe is balanced by collective restoration (Fortinbras, Malcolm).
